# The TangoTube logo

`mark.svg` is the couple in their embrace, the site's favicon. `wordmark.svg` is
the TangoTube wordmark. `internal/brand/wordmark.png` is the wordmark drawn
white on black at 2620×497; `tt` samples it to draw the name in a terminal, and
draws the couple from the geometry in `mark.svg` so they can turn.
